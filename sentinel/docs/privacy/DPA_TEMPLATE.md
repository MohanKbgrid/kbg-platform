# Data processing agreement - template

**A starting point for counsel, not a finished contract.** Between **[Customer]** (Data Fiduciary) and
**KBGrid [legal entity]** (Data Processor), for the Sentinel exception-handling service at **[site]**.

1. **Subject and duration.** KBGrid processes personal data only to install, operate, support and improve
   Sentinel for the Customer, for the term of the main agreement **[ref]**.
2. **Data and people.** Personal data in operational events the Customer's systems send to Sentinel
   (customers' names, phone numbers, addresses, order and payment references) and the Customer's staff who
   use Sentinel (names, work contacts, decisions). No sensitive categories are expected; the Customer will
   not send them without telling KBGrid in writing.
3. **Instructions.** KBGrid acts only on the Customer's documented instructions, including this agreement
   and the Sentinel configuration the Customer publishes, and tells the Customer if an instruction seems
   unlawful.
4. **Where.** Data stays on the Customer's server **[location]** / in **[region]**. Model calls go to
   **[provider, region]** with personal fields masked as configured; no prompts or replies are stored.
   Transfers outside India only as the Act and any Government notification allow.
5. **Security.** Encryption in transit; access by named people with their own tokens or single sign-on;
   two-person approval for configuration changes; an audit trail of every step; outbound address checks;
   backups that are verified; logs kept for **[one year - confirm]**.
6. **Staff.** People who can see the data are bound by confidentiality and trained.
7. **Sub-processors.** **[list: model provider, hosting]**. KBGrid gives **[30]** days' notice of a new one
   and the Customer may object.
8. **Requests from people.** KBGrid helps the Customer answer access, correction and erasure requests within
   **[n]** days, using Sentinel's privacy tools (access report, erasure, retention by data class).
9. **Breaches.** KBGrid tells the Customer without undue delay and in any case within **[24]** hours of
   becoming aware, with what it knows, and keeps helping so the Customer can notify the Data Protection
   Board and affected people on time (see the breach runbook).
10. **Retention and return.** Personal fields are removed from closed cases after **[90]** days and whole
    cases after **[365]** days, unless the Customer sets other periods. At the end, KBGrid returns or deletes
    the data, including archives and backups within **[30]** days, and confirms in writing.
11. **Audits.** The Customer may audit compliance once a year with **[30]** days' notice, or after a breach.
12. **Liability.** As in the main agreement **[ref]**.
